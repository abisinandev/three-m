CHAPTER 2 - STOCK MARKET & REGULATION
========================================

Topic:
Understanding the stock market, market participants, and SEBI regulation.

----------------------------------------
WHAT IS A STOCK MARKET?
----------------------------------------

The stock market is a place where people buy and sell shares of publicly listed companies.

Examples of companies:
- Infosys
- Reliance Industries
- TCS

Purpose of Stock Market:
- Helps investors buy shares
- Helps investors sell shares
- Connects buyers and sellers electronically

In India, stock markets operate digitally through computers and mobile applications.

Main Stock Exchanges in India:
1. Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE)
2. National Stock Exchange (NSE)

Investors cannot directly access stock exchanges. Transactions happen through registered stock brokers.

----------------------------------------
MARKET PARTICIPANTS
----------------------------------------

Anyone who buys or sells in the stock market is called a market participant.

Types of Participants:
1. Retail Investors
   - Normal individual investors

2. Domestic Institutions
   - Large Indian organizations like LIC

3. Mutual Fund Companies (AMC)
   - SBI Mutual Fund
   - HDFC AMC

4. Foreign Institutional Investors (FII)
   - Foreign investment companies and funds

5. NRIs and OCIs
   - Indians living outside India

Main Goal:
All participants aim to generate profits from investments.

----------------------------------------
WHY REGULATION IS IMPORTANT
----------------------------------------

Since money is involved, markets can be affected by:
- Greed
- Fear
- Fraud
- Manipulation
- Insider trading

To ensure fairness, stock markets require proper regulation and monitoring.

----------------------------------------
SEBI - MARKET REGULATOR
----------------------------------------

SEBI stands for:
Securities and Exchange Board of India

SEBI is the main regulator of Indian financial markets.

Main Objectives of SEBI:
1. Protect investors
2. Regulate stock exchanges
3. Prevent market manipulation
4. Ensure fair trading practices
5. Promote market development

SEBI monitors:
- Stock exchanges
- Brokers
- Mutual funds
- Listed companies
- Market participants

----------------------------------------
IMPORTANT MARKET ENTITIES
----------------------------------------

1. Stock Brokers
Help investors buy and sell shares.

Examples:
- Zerodha
- ICICI Direct
- Sharekhan

2. Depositories
Store shares electronically in DEMAT accounts.

Examples:
- NSDL
- CDSL

3. Asset Management Companies (AMC)
Manage mutual funds.

Examples:
- SBI Mutual Fund
- HDFC AMC

4. Merchant Bankers
Help companies launch IPOs.

5. Credit Rating Agencies
Evaluate financial strength of companies.

Examples:
- CRISIL
- ICRA

----------------------------------------
KEY TAKEAWAYS
----------------------------------------

1. Stock markets help investors trade shares.
2. Indian stock markets are mainly BSE and NSE.
3. Investors trade through stock brokers.
4. Many participants operate in the market.
5. SEBI regulates and protects the market.
6. Regulation helps maintain fairness and investor confidence.
