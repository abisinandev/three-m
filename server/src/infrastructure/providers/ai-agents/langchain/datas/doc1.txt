WHY SHOULD I INVEST?
- To beat inflation
- To grow wealth through compounding
- To achieve financial goals
- To secure future financial stability

WHERE SHOULD I INVEST?
1. Fixed Income (FDs, Bonds)
   - Low risk
   - Stable returns

2. Equity / Stocks
   - Higher risk
   - Higher long-term returns

3. Real Estate
   - Property investment
   - Requires larger capital

4. Gold / Commodities
   - Diversification and safety

KEY PRINCIPLE
- Diversify investments across different asset classes
