Internal Risk Score Model

Risk Score Formula:
Risk Score = (Volatility Index * 0.4) + (Debt Ratio * 0.3) + (Market Sentiment * 0.3)

Where:
- Volatility Index ranges from 0 to 100
- Debt Ratio = Total Debt / Total Assets
- Market Sentiment is derived from social signals

Risk Levels:
0–30 → Low Risk
31–60 → Moderate Risk
61–100 → High Risk
